# ListItUp brand assets

- `listitup-mark.png` is the generated standalone logo mark with a transparent background.
- `listitup-ribbon-concept-v1.png` is the alternative folded-ribbon concept.
- `listitup-ribbon-concept-v2-burgundy.png` replaces the blue fold with warm burgundy.
- `listitup-ribbon-concept-v3-porcelain.png` is the current mark used by the app; its warm porcelain-white fold is optimized for dark surfaces.
- `listitup-icon-192.png` and `listitup-icon-512.png` are installable-app sizes derived from the v3 mark.
- The earlier concepts are retained for comparison and are not referenced by app metadata.

The editable/vector production version should be redrawn from this approved visual direction before final trademark or print use.
